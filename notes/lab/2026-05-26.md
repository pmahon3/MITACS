# Session 2026-05-26 — Applied-audit workflow born; first four settled findings (S1–S8)

## What happened

Five-hour session that **created the applied-audit workflow from
scratch and immediately put it through four full cycles**. Started at
06:36 with the workflow commit (`5067ae3`: 9 agents, registry, code-path
detector); ended at 11:29 with a CLAUDE.md refresh (`c6487ec`)
documenting the new shape.

The workflow's threat model is the lab's own retraction history (per
[[mitacs-session-lessons-corpus]]: 13 of 26 prior memory files carry
RETRACTED markers). Eight named retraction modes map to specific
defenses: code-path audit for diagnostic-reimplements-production,
preregister for argmin-without-variance and in-sample reading,
multiverse for cosmetic-vs-mechanistic, framework-escalator for
framework-vs-parameter, arbiter for single-session momentum,
pre-experiment for registered-experiment mutation, and the registry's
hash chain for provenance drift. The eighth (pre-registered-criterion-
vs-legitimate-data-structure) didn't have a name yet — three
instances later in the session (eigenmodes O2, P1 tautology, Q1 ν
saturation) it would become the fourth named mode that the arbiter
pattern *handles* rather than prevents.

**First cycle: multiscale-direct-h-step (`44f0944`)**. PROVISIONAL —
the M1 metric was methodologically degenerate at large h (the iterated-
factor norm denominator collapses to ~0 at large h, inflating the
ratio without dynamical content). Real first lesson: the workflow's
preregistration step catches "method too crude to discriminate"
before it produces a finding.

**Second cycle: v2 of multiscale-direct-h-step (`b350a17`, `f7fb669`,
`d255109`, `c76fb71`)**. v2 redefined the metric as a distributional
divergence (KL/Wasserstein) on the scalar innovation rather than a
norm ratio. **SETTLED**: D_RATIO = 28.96 [26.82, 31.43] at the
headline cell (weekday, h=12). The strict-semigroup picture (A) is
empirically inadequate by 29× the synthetic VAR(1) noise floor;
picture (C) — relaxed factorization with cross-scale coherence
defect — is corroborated under the Gaussian kernel. **(S1) and (S6)**
in the memo's master numbering. First SETTLED entry in the registry.

A small but consequential workflow defect surfaced and was fixed
(`2ce42c7`): `registry.stamp` was treating the artifact-being-stamped
as a source change, making `git_clean: false` on its own creation.
The fix exempts the artifact's own path; documented in
`preregister.md` (`c58f984`).

**Third cycle: eigenmode characterization of v2's defect (`6e3cc28`,
`01d5ea2`)**. SETTLED MIXED. Mechanical O2 fired on a near-tie
technicality (top eigenmode 0.99·second-mode); substantive picture is
O3 (defect diffuse across two modes), refuting the proponent's
single-mode forecast. Asymmetry sub-test: R_sat/R_wkd ratios 1.3–2.4
vs library expectation 5.0 → weekday per-cell defect EXCEEDS noise-
only prediction (genuine dynamical asymmetry, NOT library artifact).
Rank-1 ill-posedness concern empirically refuted by synthetic gate.
**(S2) and (S3)**. First instance of the fourth named failure mode
(mechanical/substantive gap due to pre-registered criterion firing on
legitimate-but-unanticipated data structure).

**Threads land (`7bda6fa`, `6d90dd5`, `30c2347`)**. The workflow gets
its multi-experiment unit: a thread is a pre-registered tree of
experiments whose branching rules pin which experiment fires next
based on the previous arbiter's verdict. Silent post-hoc pivoting at
the line-of-inquiry level becomes detectable. State advancement is
recorded in `state_history` with `prior_hash` for provenance.

**Fourth cycle: missing-content thread P1 (`b14434e`, `217b091`)**.
First live use of the thread workflow. P1 = PIT calendar fingerprint:
test 15 calendar candidates for conditional PIT explanation of the
iterated κ_1 forecast residual. SETTLED AMBIGUOUS — mechanically the
top-seasonal/top-weekly ratio fell outside the O-C corroboration
band, AND substantively the top two candidates were within-cell
residuals of `z_actual` (tautology by construction). Three durable
findings nonetheless: (a) marginal PIT pathologically U-shaped (χ² =
22,329; Gaussian forecast assumption EMPIRICALLY WRONG, 67% of PIT
mass in extreme bins); (b) excluding the tautological candidates,
`year_since_cutoff` (secular drift) is the strongest legitimate
signal (corroborates [[mitacs-clim-gap-nonstationary]]); (c) day-
type-specific bias pattern (sunday under-forecast, weekday/saturday
over-forecast). **(S4) and (S5)**. Thread `missing-content-thread`
transitions active → exhausted under registered branching (P1
AMBIGUOUS → null per branching_rules; continuing requires amendment).

**Memo rewrite (`84aa3d0`, `5f0e357`, `42d52e7`)**. The
multiscale-factor-coherence seed was Gaussian-implicit. The
SETTLED-AMBIGUOUS P1 verdict (chi² = 22,329 marginal PIT under the
iterated Gaussian forecast) makes the Gaussian assumption itself the
load-bearing problem. The memo gets rewritten as kernel-family-
agnostic: defines relaxed factorization without committing to a
particular kernel; adds a Student-t section with full math; adds a
new thread `distributional-class-thread` whose Q1 lifts the kernel
family from Gaussian to Student-t.

**Fifth cycle: distributional-class Q1 (`83e9fe0`, `dfacc1b`,
`06eaefd`, `920fcbc`, `f71be6d`)**. Q1 fits a Student-t kernel
(M1: one global ν per day-type) and a Student-t-full-commitment
predictor (M2: M1's kernel + Student-t climatology per (m, h) bin).
SETTLED R-D mechanical / R-C substantive (the second instance of the
fourth named failure mode):

- **M1 reduces χ² by 16×** (22,329 → 1,371). Real effect, large.
- **M2 underperforms M1.** Heavy-tailedness lives in the κ_1
  innovation, not the seasonal climatology — refining a Student-t
  climatology adds noise without capturing real signal. (M2 ν

  demand is approximately Gaussian by CLT-like aggregation — this
  is what fired R-D mechanically.)
- **D_RATIO_t = 2.93 [2.62, 3.24]** at the headline cell. v2's
  D_RATIO = 28.96 shrinks 10× under the corrected kernel — ~90% of
  v2's headline magnitude was kernel-family-conditional artifact;
  **v2's SETTLED finding requires an asterisk on magnitude.** (S8c).
- ν per day-type 4.19 / 4.62 / 4.61 — matching prediction from
  excess kurtosis 24–33 → ν ≈ 4 + 6/kurt ≈ 4.5 (the
  [[mitacs-rebaseline-facts]] in-sample finding holds out-of-sample

- **(S8)** in the memo's master numbering.

## Decisions

- **Adopt the applied-audit workflow as the standard for any
  experiment that will produce a citable finding.** Eight-step
  lifecycle: preregister → DA simultaneous → code-path (BLOCKING) →
  phase-fidelity Check P (L1 BLOCKING; L2 advisory) → run → Check R
  (BLOCKING) → arbiter (sole writer of SETTLED) → thread-coordinator
  (sole writer of thread.yaml). Documented in `notes/seeds/
  applied_audit_workflow.md` and `.claude/agents/`. Registry at
  `notes/preregistrations/` with hash chain.

- **The fourth named failure mode (pre-registered-criterion-vs-
  legitimate-data-structure) is handled by the arbiter pattern, not
  by engineering prevention.** Three instances this session
  (eigenmodes O2, P1 tautology, Q1 ν saturation). The arbiter's
  mechanical-verdict-plus-substantive-content shape is the
  workflow's correct response; no engineering fix attempted.

- **(S1) and (S6) v2 multiscale-direct-h-step SETTLED with
  asterisk.** D_RATIO = 28.96 is real but kernel-family-conditional;
  under the corrected Student-t kernel it shrinks 10× to 2.93. The
  v2 finding stands as evidence for picture (C) but the headline
  magnitude requires the asterisk. (S8c) qualifier added in the memo.

- **(S2)/(S3) eigenmodes SETTLED MIXED.** The defect is diffuse
  across two modes (not single-mode as proponent forecast); weekday/
  weekend asymmetry is structural, not library-noise.

- **(S4)/(S5) PIT-calendar-fingerprint SETTLED AMBIGUOUS.** Marginal
  PIT pathologically non-uniform (χ² = 22,329); secular drift is the
  strongest legitimate calendar signal. Tautology design hole logged
  for future P1-class experiments.

- **(S8) Q1 distributional-class SETTLED R-D mechanical / R-C
  substantive.** Student-t kernel reduces marginal χ² by 16× but
  does NOT fully calibrate; v2's headline magnitude requires the
  asterisk that ~90% was distributional-class artifact.

- **Memo is now kernel-family-agnostic.** The relaxed-factorization
  picture (C) is defined without commitment to Gaussian; Student-t
  is a worked-out concrete case. This supports the new
  `distributional-class-thread`.

- **`global_ols_fit` added to production** (`processing/innovations/
  estimator.py`). The workflow forced the discipline — the
  multiscale-factor experiment would have flagged REIMPLEMENTED
  otherwise. First instance of "the audit workflow forced a
  promotion to production code that was previously inline scratch."
