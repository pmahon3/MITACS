information).

**Stage 1 — Orientation.** Read
`~/Research/Mathematics/Resolvent_Framework/notes/unsorted/disintegration_diagnostic.md`
(parked 2026-05-26; the identifiability-obstruction theorem at
level 1) and its audit correction (2026-05-27; killed the

**Stage 2 — Applied-side literature scout.** Dispatched
general-purpose agent in background to scout whether "level 3 =
necessary-condition extraction from within-stratum residual
structure" is published methodology or survivable novelty.
Scout searched seven traditions (misspecification testing,
sensitivity analysis, causal discovery with latents, variance

Andrews-Mallows / scale-mixture). Verdict: **PARTIALLY PUBLISHED.**
The canonical match is **Diggle 1988 *Biometrics* 44: 959-971** and
**Diggle-Heagerty-Liang-Zeger 2002 ch. 5** — three-component
covariance decomposition (τ² nugget + σ²ρ(u) serial + ν² random
intercept) is algebraically exact for what the within-stratum
diagnostic computes; the "necessary conditions on Z" framing is a
contrapositive translation of what Diggle's model structure
already licenses. Scout flagged second-pass audit hook (Diggle
1988 main text via JSTOR not webfetch-readable).

**Stage 4 — Cross-session coordination.** User flagged that the

**Stage 5 — Disagreement resolution.** Framework session confirmed:
**Diggle provides the machinery but not the inferential inversion.**

the forward citation network (Verbeke-Lesaffre 1998; Heagerty

on **PARTIALLY PUBLISHED with the surviving novelty narrowed to:**
(a) inferential inversion as framing; (b) four-level hierarchy
with theorem-bounded limits at levels 1 and 4; (c) unification
of scattered components into a single programme.
