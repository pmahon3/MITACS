## Decisions

- **Lock S9 + S10 as joint applied evidence for a Z-identification
  obstruction.** Independently each is an inconclusive R-B2 / R-C3
  verdict; jointly they form the strongest applied witness so far that
  the conditional-PIT defect does not reduce to a sufficient σ-algebra
  in either the distributional or aggregate-decomposition direction
  tested. This reading depends on the framework's disintegration
  apparatus and is recorded as a memo §6 reframe, not a registry
  claim.

- **Close `distributional-class-thread` as EXHAUSTED rather than amend
  a third time.** Two amendments (A1 closed Q2D, A2 reopened Q2B) are
  the registered envelope. A third amendment to chase a finer
  decomposition would be silent-pivot territory; the right shape is a
  new thread on the framework-prescribed paths, which is what
  `resolution-paths-thread` does.

- **Use plain Patra-Sen (Patra & Sen 2016) for P1, not NPMLEmix.** The
  scout established NPMLEmix exists and is the appropriate tool for the
  corrected (ii) direction, but NPMLEmix depends on Mosek (commercial
  solver). Plain Patra-Sen has a clean R / Python implementation and is
  sufficient for P1's per-stratum localization question. NPMLEmix is
  deferred to whichever node, if any, requires the joint mixture fit.

- **Branch to `lab-notebook` for the unified-UI work.** The MVP is
  scoped narrowly (read-only Dash browser over existing surfaces, no
  edits to settled artifacts). Whether it merges back to `operator`
  depends on its real utility over a few sessions of use.
