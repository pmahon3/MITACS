## resolution-paths-thread

### The question

This thread asks which of the three framework-prescribed resolution paths closes the residual $\chi^2 \approx 954$ of the $\kappa_1$ predictor on post-cutoff Ontario data, where "closure" is defined as cumulative explained share $> 0.80$ against the Q2A baseline. The thread's scope is narrow by design: it tests the $\sigma$-algebra-enrichment axis with auxiliary information $Z$, and it does **not** re-test the distributional-class axis — that lever was bounded by [2026-05-26_q2a-richer-family] (S9) and [2026-05-26_q2b-non-distributional-decomposition] (S10). The framework grounding is the identifiability obstruction parked at `~/Research/Mathematics/Resolvent_Framework/notes/unsorted/disintegration_diagnostic.md` (with its `disintegration_diagnostic_audit_correction.md` companion): per Bergna et al. (2026, Prop 1), Heckman & Singer (1984), and Allahverdyan (2020), structured-noise and noisy-structure are observationally indistinguishable from $\kappa_Q$ alone. The joint S9+S10 verdict is the applied confirmation of that obstruction; resolution requires breaking the tie via information outside $\kappa_Q$, i.e. enriching the conditioning $\sigma$-algebra. All results are attribution-grade — adding $Z$ to a predictor changes its conditioning $\sigma$-algebra, so this thread does not yield deployable forecasts but rather a Bayes-risk decomposition of the residual non-Gaussianity.

### The tree and the rules

Five nodes were planned initially; **amendment A1 (2026-05-27)** added a sixth, **Q1A'**, as a non-gating sibling of Q1A under P1.R-A. **P1** (Patra–Sen per stratum) runs first as a localization diagnostic: for each of five natural stratification axes (demand quantile, time-of-day, season, day-type, hour-of-week), compute the plain Patra & Sen (2016) lower bound $\hat\alpha_L$ per stratum on the standardized residuals of Q1's M1 (Student-t kernel) predictor, with 1000 paired-day bootstrap CIs. The verdict statistic is $\max_{\text{axis}} \mathrm{range}(\hat\alpha_L)$: R-A at $> 0.20$ (localizable), R-B in $(0.10, 0.20]$, R-C at $< 0.10$ (orthogonal). Localize-first is deliberate — per the framework prescription, candidate-$Z$ testing without first checking that $Z$ is concentrable on natural strata wastes signal. **Q1A/Q1B** re-fit M3 (mixture-2-Gaussian) with $Z$ derived from P1's strongest (resp. second-strongest) axis under R-A/R-B; **Q1C** tests temperature as a prior-supported fallback under R-C, motivated by `mitacs-clim-gap-nonstationary` and `mitacs-postcovid-probe`. **P2** is a synthetic-Gaussian-DGP calibration backstop, fired only if all live R-A branches collapse to R-C1A/B/C. **Q1A'** runs *in parallel* with Q1A as a data-driven Z-recovery consistency check (SIR vs FastICA head-to-head); its verdict is independent and does not advance the thread state machine. Q1A remains the gating node for `current_node_id` progression. The closure rule (for the gating sequence) is numeric, following Q2B's convention: $$\text{share} = \frac{\chi^2_{\text{base}} - \chi^2_{\text{after}}}{\chi^2_{\text{base}} - \chi^2_{\text{R-A2 cut}}},$$ with cumulative share $> 0.80$ across tested $Z$'s $\Rightarrow$ RESOLVED, otherwise $\Rightarrow$ EXHAUSTED-UNRESOLVED. The methods-novelty branch was determined dead by `notes/literature/2026-05-26_stratified-patra-sen-scout.md`: Deb, Saha, Guntuboyina & Sen (2022, JASA; NPMLEmix) provide the canonical covariate-dependent generalization, so this thread uses plain Patra–Sen per stratum — sufficient for the registered verdict without invoking NPMLEmix's Mosek dependency.
