## resolution-paths-thread

### The question

This thread asks which of the three framework-prescribed resolution paths closes the residual $\chi^2 \approx 954$ of the $\kappa_1$ predictor on post-cutoff Ontario data, where "closure" is defined as cumulative explained share $> 0.80$ against the Q2A baseline. The thread's scope is narrow by design: it tests the $\sigma$-algebra-enrichment axis with auxiliary information $Z$, and it does **not** re-test the distributional-class axis — that lever was bounded by [2026-05-26_q2a-richer-family] (S9) and [2026-05-26_q2b-non-distributional-decomposition] (S10). The framework grounding is the identifiability obstruction parked at `~/Research/Mathematics/Resolvent_Framework/notes/unsorted/disintegration_diagnostic.md` (with its `disintegration_diagnostic_audit_correction.md` companion): per Bergna et al. (2026, Prop 1), Heckman & Singer (1984), and Allahverdyan (2020), structured-noise and noisy-structure are observationally indistinguishable from $\kappa_Q$ alone. The joint S9+S10 verdict is the applied confirmation of that obstruction; resolution requires breaking the tie via information outside $\kappa_Q$, i.e. enriching the conditioning $\sigma$-algebra. All results are attribution-grade — adding $Z$ to a predictor changes its conditioning $\sigma$-algebra, so this thread does not yield deployable forecasts but rather a Bayes-risk decomposition of the residual non-Gaussianity.
